## German suited deck (`german/`) – mariášové karty

- Author: **Gunter Gerhardt**, originally drawn for [XSkat](http://xskat.de/)
  (`cardset-xskat-german-large.zip`), uploaded to Wikimedia Commons.
- Source: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Playing_card-german-hart-10.svg>
  (and the other `Playing_card-german-*.svg` files)
- License: **[CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)**
- Changes: files renamed to `<suit>-<rank>.svg`
  (`hart`→`srdce`, `blatt`→`listy`, `schellen`→`kule`, `eichel`→`zaludy`;
  `unter/jack`→`U`, `ober/queen`→`O`, `konig/king`→`K`, `daus`→`A`). Content unchanged.
  Under the ShareAlike terms, any modified version of these images must also be
  released under CC BY-SA 3.0 (or a compatible license).
